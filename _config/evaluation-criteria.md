# Evaluation Criteria & Weights

These weights determine how the five scoring dimensions combine into a composite priority score. They reflect strategic priorities set during onboarding.

**This fork is configured for Clief Notes using a custom Beginner content engagement profile** (Content-driven lean, tuned for an admin + two mods shipping beginner articles).

## Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Impact | 0.30 | How many beginners this unblocks, and how deeply |
| Feasibility | 0.20 | How quickly the mod team can ship it as an article |
| Channel Alignment | 0.30 | Fit to beginner ICM article pillars and engagement goals |
| Research Alignment | 0.10 | How well this demonstrates or advances the MWP/ICM thesis (kept light; teaching first) |
| Novelty | 0.10 | How differentiated this is from existing Foundation / Skool material |

## Weight Guidelines

Weights must sum to 1.0. Available profiles (reference):

| Priority Profile | Impact | Feasibility | Channel | Research | Novelty |
|-----------------|--------|-------------|---------|----------|---------|
| **Balanced** | 0.30 | 0.20 | 0.20 | 0.20 | 0.10 |
| **Community-first** | 0.40 | 0.25 | 0.15 | 0.10 | 0.10 |
| **Research-driven** | 0.20 | 0.15 | 0.20 | 0.35 | 0.10 |
| **Content-driven** | 0.25 | 0.15 | 0.35 | 0.15 | 0.10 |
| **Ship fast** | 0.25 | 0.35 | 0.20 | 0.10 | 0.10 |
| **Beginner content engagement** (active) | 0.30 | 0.20 | 0.30 | 0.10 | 0.10 |

Why this profile: the mod team’s near-term job is engagement via beginner articles. Channel fit and beginner impact lead; feasibility stays high enough for a weekly cadence; research and novelty stay secondary so we don’t over-rank clever-but-advanced topics.

When scoring article ideas, interpret dimensions as:
- **Impact** — Unblocks many beginners / reduces a common stuck point
- **Feasibility** — Outline + draft fit in one mod session
- **Channel Alignment** — Maps to a beginner pillar above; teachable without advanced prerequisites
- **Research Alignment** — Honest ICM/MWP teaching, not forced paper citations
- **Novelty** — Fresh angle vs. repeating The Foundation verbatim

## How Weights Are Applied

```
Composite = (Impact × W_impact) + (Feasibility × W_feasibility) + (Channel × W_channel) + (Research × W_research) + (Novelty × W_novelty)
```

Maximum possible composite score: 5.0
Minimum: 1.0

## Strategic Override

The mod team can override any ranking at the Stage 04 checkpoint. Overrides are noted in the output with rationale. The scoring provides a starting point for discussion, not a final answer.
