# Announcement / Pointer Post Brief Template

**Content type:** 2 — Community announcement directing members to a classroom article

```markdown
### Announcement: [Working title / hook]

**Content type:** Announcement / pointer post
**Points to article:** [Classroom article title or brief ID]
**Owner:** [usually same as article]
**Voice file:** `shared/voices/[owner].md`
**Draft by:** [date — usually same day as article publish]

**Why post this in the feed:** [One sentence — who should click and why now]
**CTA:** [Read the classroom article / try X / reply with Y]

**Beats (keep short):**
1. Hook in community language (stuck point or win)
2. What the article gives them (one outcome)
3. Link + clear CTA
4. Optional: one question to seed comments

**Must not:** Teach the whole lesson in the announcement. Point, don't replace.
**Drafting rule:** Load only the owner's voice file.
```
