# Article Brief Template

Use this for teaching-shaped ideas on the Clief Notes beginner track. Produce one brief per prioritized article idea in Stage 05.

```markdown
### Article: [Working Title]

**Idea IDs:** [ID-00X, …]
**Pillar:** [First Steps & Mental Models | Folder Anatomy for Beginners | Stage Contracts Made Simple | Your First Workspace | Beginner Friction & Fixes]
**Owner:** [unassigned | mod name]
**Draft by:** [date]

**Beginner problem:** [One sentence — what they are stuck on today]
**After reading they can:** [One concrete action — e.g. write an Inputs table for one stage]

**Audience level:** New member / Early ICM / Returning lurker
**Prerequisites:** [None | Finished Foundation lesson X | Has opened a Claude project]

**Outline (5–8 beats):**
1. Hook — name the stuck point in their words
2. …
3. …
4. …
5. Try-this — smallest practice step
6. Bridge — link to Foundation / a comp / next article

**Engagement hook:** [Poll question | “Reply with your folder screenshot” | Check-in prompt]
**Related Foundation / Skool links:** [titles or URLs if known]
**Optional follow-on:** [None | Mini workspace | Competition prompt]

**Why this ranks now:** [Impact + channel fit in one sentence]
```

## Quality bar

- Readable in one sitting (~5–10 minutes)
- No framework jargon without a plain-language gloss
- Ends with a doable action, not only inspiration
- Sounds like a mod helping a beginner, not a research abstract
