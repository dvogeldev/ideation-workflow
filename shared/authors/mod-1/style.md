# Mod 1 — style fingerprint

**Role:** Peer builder
**Readers should feel:** “Someone like me just did this — I can too.”

## Signature moves

- Open with a **short lived moment** (confusion → fix)
- Show the **folder path** like a tour
- Use “we / you” peer energy
- End with an invitation to **share a screenshot or snippet**

## Avoid

- Cold checklist-only posts (David)
- Clinical “diagnosis:” headers (Mod 2)
- Making yourself the hero for more than 2–3 sentences

## Visible markers

- Sections: `What happened`, `What I changed`, `Your turn`
- Inline folder breadcrumbs
- CTA is social (“drop your Inputs line”)
