# Channel Alignment

Configured for **Clief Notes** (https://www.skool.com/cliefnotes).

**Operating context:** An admin and two moderators use this workspace to prioritize **beginner-focused ICM articles** that raise engagement. Idea batches may include Skool posts, Discord questions, DMs, competition confusion, and “I don’t get X” themes — not only product feature requests. Each writer drafts in their own brand voice (`shared/voices/`).

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Description | Audience Fit |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| First Steps & Mental Models | Plain-language explanations of what ICM/MWP is, why folders beat opaque agent frameworks for sequential work, and what “good” looks like | Lowest barrier; meets brand-new members where they are |
| Folder Anatomy for Beginners | Walkthroughs of `CLAUDE.md`, root `CONTEXT.md`, numbered stages, `references/`, `output/`, and `_config/` with screenshots of structure not jargon | Turns the Foundation course into skimmable reference articles |
| Stage Contracts Made Simple | Inputs / Process / Outputs, checkpoints, and audits — how to write a contract a beginner can follow | Unlocks the skill comps and templates actually require |
| Your First Workspace | Guided “build one small pipeline” articles: pick a tiny workflow, name stages, run once, edit between stages | Converts lurkers into posters who ship something |
| Beginner Friction & Fixes | Recurring stuck points (too much context, no checkpoint, summarizing instead of contracting, skipping edit surfaces) with concrete repairs | High engagement: mirrors real questions mods already see |

## Target Audience

- **Primary audience:** New and early Clief Notes members who are beginner-to-intermediate with ICM/MWP — curious about AI agents, not yet fluent in folder architecture
- **What they care about:** Clear next steps, short articles they can finish, examples they can copy, and confidence to post work or enter a competition
- **What they already know:** General AI chat tools; programming skill varies; ICM vocabulary is new or partial
- **What makes them engage:** Beginner articles that answer a real stuck point, check-ins on lessons, comps with fixed output shapes, and peer examples that look achievable

## Mod Team

- **Team:** 1 admin + 2 moderators
- **Job of this pipeline:** Turn community confusion and idea scraps into a ranked backlog of **beginner articles** (and only secondarily into MWP workspace builds or software)
- **Default deliverable shape:** Article brief → outline → Skool post in the owner's brand voice (optional follow-on: mini workspace or competition tie-in)
- **Voices:** `shared/voices/david.md`, `shared/voices/mod-1.md`, `shared/voices/mod-2.md` (rename placeholders when real docs are dropped in)
- **Handoff:** Ranked briefs should be assignable across the three of you without re-explaining audience or pillars

## Strategic Goals

- **Channel mission:** Give builders the cliff notes on the new AI age by teaching Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM / MWP) — folder structure as agent architecture — through the Skool course, biweekly competitions, and shared drop-in workspaces
- **Engagement mission (this fork):** Publish a steady stream of beginner ICM articles that reduce drop-off after onboarding and pull lurkers into discussion, comps, and first builds
- **Research thesis:** The MWP/ICM methodology — folder structure as agent architecture — replaces framework-level orchestration for sequential, human-reviewed workflows
- **Community value:** Articles that unblock beginners compound into more competition entries, better questions, and reusable templates
- **Content flywheel:** Beginner stuck point → article → discussion/check-in → optional mini workspace or comp prompt → showcase
- **Current community need:** Beginners want ICM applied to *their* work but stall on mental models, folder anatomy, and writing a first stage contract — they need short teaching pieces before more advanced templates
- **Community domains:** software development, content creation, consulting, education, research, marketing, operations, sales/support workflows

## Alignment Test

When evaluating whether an idea aligns with the channel, ask:

1. **Does this become a beginner article?** — Can we ship a clear Skool post (or series) a new member can finish in one sitting?
2. **Does it unblock the next action?** — After reading, can they rename a folder, write an Inputs table, or run one stage — not just “understand the vibe”?
3. **Does it reinforce the thesis simply?** — Does it show folders/markdown as the orchestration, without requiring framework literacy?
4. **Will it spark engagement?** — Comments, check-ins, “I tried this,” or a natural bridge into a competition?
5. **Is it shareable inside Clief Notes?** — Would a mod confidently drop it in onboarding or a stuck-member reply?
